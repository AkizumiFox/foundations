**Brief:** PIPELINE S2a, plus the coordinator's list: each schema is exactly the rule; every sequent is derivable when claimed, using only the rules introduced by then; every "needs RAA" claim is proved, or stated without proof and not relied on; the route of `thm-classical-rules-interderivable`; the search method's safe-step claims; the √2/√3/√6/√4 facts; exercise answers and marking schemes; exit checks that only recall printed proofs; label bookkeeping; closeness to Huth & Ryan §1.2 (converted sections 004–007 and the PDF's Exercises 1.2, pp. 79–80); no return of problems the Chapter 1 reviews fixed.

**Method.**
- **Derivations.** Every derivation the blueprint marks "scratch N…" (the S1 scratch file is not in the repo) and every replacement below was written as a fitch-book `nd` block and checked with `python3 tools/check_fitch.py`: 35 blocks, 0 errors. The two `% expect-invalid` blocks (§01 C2 and its "proves too much" version) fail exactly at line 4, as the blueprint says; this was checked by stripping the marker. The D-numbers the blueprint reuses were checked against the D-file (76 blocks, 0 errors), and every line count it quotes was compared with the file.
- **Schemas.** `check_fitch.py authoring/blueprints/ch02.md`: 9 blocks, 0 errors. The tool does not check the shape of lines written with \(\varphi\), so each schema was read by hand against its rule, and the instance blocks S1–S3 (letters for \(\varphi, \psi\)) were machine-checked.
- **"Needs RAA" claims.** A script (scratch `kripke.py`, results quoted) searched rooted Kripke frames with up to 4 worlds. The primitive rules without RAA are intuitionistic natural deduction, which is sound for Kripke models. So a classically valid sequent with a Kripke counter-model has no derivation without RAA. The script also evaluated every sequent in Chapter 3's three-valued Gödel semantics (the method of `thm-raa-independent`) and by truth table.
- **Chapter 1 and later chapters.** Cited Chapter 1 facts were checked against `ch01.md`: \(\vee\)E's "same last line", the scope rule and (D3) of `def-derivation`, `def-rule-schema`, and the rule that no derived rule is used before Chapter 2 §03. Positions and labels that other files rely on were grepped in `src/ch11-least-upper-bound/`, `ch03.md`–`ch08.md` and `STATUS.md`. `thm-raa-independent`'s statement was read in `ch03.md` l. 144, and `ch05.md` §07 was read for overlaps.
- **Earlier reviews.** Every fixed item in `ch01-S2.md`, `-S5-A.md`, `-S5-B.md`, `-S6.md`, `-S7-recheck.md` and `-S8-seam.md` that concerns the old §04–§07 was grepped for in this blueprint: Glivenko, "RAA never needed for a negation", the costume, the unmarked "needs RAA", the H&R sequents, and the \(\bot\)E remark.
