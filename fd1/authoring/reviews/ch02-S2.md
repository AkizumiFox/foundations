# Chapter 2 — S2a statement referee report

**Blueprint:** `authoring/blueprints/ch02.md` (as of 2026-09-28, before this review's edits; line numbers below refer to the pre-edit file).
**Referee brief:** PIPELINE S2a, plus: check the definitions (formulas, parse trees, unique readability, valuations, ⊨), the induction chain (`ax-induction` ⇒ strong ⇒ structural), the soundness route (contexts and boxes), the completeness route (signed atoms, LEM), CNF/DNF and adequacy, every exercise answer and marking scheme, every derivation, pedagogy, and overlap with Huth & Ryan §1.3–1.5 (examples and exercises, not ideas).

**Method.**
- **Derivations.** Every sequent the blueprint calls derivable, and every replacement proposed below, was written as a fitch-book `nd` block and checked with `tools/check_fitch.py`. The file is `authoring/reviews/ch02-S2-derivations.md`: 27 blocks, 0 errors, 7 notes (lines justified by `\by{…}`: LEM, DS, a cited row of `lem-connective-rows`, a pasted derivation). The letters \(A, B\) stand for the arbitrary formulas \(\alpha, \beta\) of `lem-connective-rows`. As a sanity check, a copy of C9 with one wrong citation was correctly rejected ("¬E needs a statement and its negation").
- **Semantics.** A script (`sem.py`, scratch; the results are quoted below) checked the following:
  - every rule of the calculus, in the context form "\(\min\Gamma_k \le v(\varphi_k)\)", against the three-valued semantics of `thm-raa-independent`, over all \(3^4\) value combinations;
  - the tautologies and refuting rows claimed in §03–§07;
  - the closure of \(\{\neg,\leftrightarrow\}\) on two atoms (8 functions, T-row counts 0, 2, 4 only);
  - the closure of \(\{\neg,\mathsf M\}\) on three atoms (16 functions, all self-dual, \(p\wedge q\) not among them);
