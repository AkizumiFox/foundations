# Chapter 3 — S2a statement referee report

**Blueprint:** `authoring/blueprints/ch03.md` (as of 2026-09-28, before this review's edits).
**Referee brief:** PIPELINE S2a, plus: the syntax (terms, free and bound variables, substitution, capture); the ∀/∃/= rules and their side conditions, each tested for being exactly strong enough by a counterexample to the weakened rule; every quantifier law claimed derivable (derived) or not (counter-model); the centrepiece UC ⇒ C and the failure of its converse; the models section; the optional soundness route (§09); every exercise answer and marking scheme; pedagogy; overlap with Huth & Ryan ch. 2 (converted sections 022–028 and the exercises 034–036).

**Method.**
- **Derivations.** `check_fitch.py` does not check quantifier or equality lines (it notes them), so every derivation below was written out and checked by hand, line by line, against the rules *as the blueprint states them*, side conditions included. Line counts are for the book's conventions (premises numbered, one line per rule application, bounded-quantifier rules of §07 as derived rules where the blueprint says so).
- **Side conditions.** For each condition I dropped it alone and looked for a derivation with a true premise and a false conclusion in a concrete structure (ℕ, ℤ, a two- or three-element model). For each I also checked the soundness case of §09 uses it, and that nothing stronger is used (so the condition is not stronger than needed).
- **"Needs RAA" claims.** Each was checked for classical validity and refuted intuitionistically by a Kripke model with growing domains (e.g. for \( \neg\forall x P \vdash \exists x \neg P \): \( w_0 \) with domain \( \{a\} \), \( w_1 \ge w_0 \) with \( \{a, b\} \), \( P(a) \) forced everywhere, \( P(b) \) nowhere). The book cannot prove these claims (see #28).
- **Finite searches.** The §06 C1 example (\( \exists!x\exists!y \) vs \( \exists!y\exists!x \)) was found by exhaustive search over all relations on 1, 2 and 3 elements (`scratchpad/uniq.py`): none on ≤ 2 elements. The other finite models were checked by hand.
- **Rendering.** A guarded derivation (`\open[y_0] \open[x_0] …`) was compiled with `latex/fitch-book.sty` (pdflatex, clean) and inspected. The same derivation run through `tools/check_fitch.py` fails (#6).
- **Huth & Ryan.** Every sequent, example and exercise in the blueprint was compared with H&R §2.2.3–2.4 (Examples 2.9–2.12, the text derivations of §2.3.1, Theorem 2.13 and its proofs) and Exercises 2.3 (1–15), 2.4 and 2.5.
- **Gate.** `.venv/bin/python blueprint/check_blueprint.py 3`: 0 problems before and after the edits.
