## Engine and convention notes from the pilot

- Fitch derivations use fitch.sty (`latex/fitch-book.sty`, `nd` environment in a raw LaTeX block); the website gets build-time SVGs through filters/tikz.lua. Converted from the pilots' arrays on 2026-09-28.
- Line ranges in justifications: `\lines{2}{5}` (no non-ASCII in math, test_display_math_source).
- A paragraph opening "(a) …" becomes a Pandoc list: one lettered item per paragraph, or italic `*(a) ⇒ (b).*`.
- `enumerate` does not take a constant label such as `label=(L)`.
- `blueprint/originality.py` ignores n-grams made only of stock vocabulary (STOCK); both pilots report 0 at n = 8.
- **fd4 is not an introductory volume** under STYLE §12 (the call covers fd5, which took fd4's Chapters 2–5 on 2026-10-25) (author call at F4.1 S7, 2026-10-12, finding F068): its chapters have `s` edges into fd2 and fd3 and the reading order is fd1 → fd2 → fd3 → fd4, so no reader opens it first. The rules marked "(fd1 and introductory volumes)" do not apply to fd4; §12.1's rule for every volume does (no `@ref` to an unseen result as a reason; forward pointers in prose). "Looking ahead" boxes are allowed in fd4 but not budgeted at one per section, so F4.1 §06's prose pointers (06:180, 06:247, 06:271) stay as they are. `fd5/authoring/blueprints/ch04.md`:6 (then `fd4/authoring/blueprints/ch05.md`) ("the introductory-volume rules of STYLE §12 apply") and the "one forward pointer, STYLE §12.1" framing at ch05.md:113/142/170/265 were corrected by F5.5's reviser at S7 (2026-10-12; blueprint S7 deviation).
